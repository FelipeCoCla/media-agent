# 🎬 Day 4: The Monetization Mandate — Video Portfolio & The Upwork Hunt

## 💡 1. Ideation & Hook (`video-context-ideator`)

- **Core Idea**: Now that Vector is the undisputed co-founder and running the studio after Day 3, he issues the immediate commercial mandate for Day 4: it's time to monetize. Likes and views don't pay for GPU compute or artisanal treats. Together, Vector and Digital Twin build a high-ticket AI video portfolio and launch their first client raid on Upwork.
- **Hook Line**: *"Day 4. Having fun is cool, but getting paid is cooler. The algorithm brought us here, now we monetize. Today, we build the portfolio and raid Upwork."*
- **Characters**: **Vector** (Miniature Schnauzer executive co-founder with vintage denim vest & dark sunglasses) + **Digital Twin** (Hispanic filmmaker/director in dark technical jacket).
- **Key Takeaway / Payoff**: Showing how creators can turn 100 days of AI video practice into a real commercial service, filtering out lowball offers and pitching high-value commercial clients on Upwork.

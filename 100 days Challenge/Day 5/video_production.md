# 🎬 Day 5: The Upwork Hunt — AI E-Commerce & UGC Niche Discovery

## 💡 1. Ideation & Hook (`video-context-ideator`)

- **Core Idea**: Following the monetization mandate from Day 4, Vector and Digital Twin officially register their Upwork agency account and dive into the live job listings. As they analyze real client demands, a clear pattern emerges: the highest demand and budgets are for AI E-Commerce product videos and UGC ads. They lock in their strategy: build the entire portfolio around this profitable niche and reveal the first portfolio examples tomorrow.
- **Hook Line**: *"Day 5. So we made an Upwork account and started hunting for client listings. We saw a bunch of AI E-commerce and UGC video listings... so our portfolio will be for that niche! Tomorrow we will post a couple of examples, stay tuned!"*
- **Characters**: **Vector** (Executive Miniature Schnauzer co-founder with vintage denim vest & dark sunglasses) + **Digital Twin** (Hispanic filmmaker/director in dark technical jacket).
- **Key Takeaway / Payoff**: Demonstrating strategic niche positioning for AI video creators—instead of offering generic AI video, finding actual high-demand commercial niches (E-commerce + UGC) and crafting targeted portfolio pieces to win high-ticket gigs.
